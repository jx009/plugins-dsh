## The short version

{brand} ({siteUrl}) is a static website. There are no accounts, no sign-up forms and no comment system. We do not collect personal profiles, and we have no database of visitors.

## What is stored in your browser

The site saves interface preferences in your browser's localStorage. At present that means your theme choice. This stays on your device, is not sent to us, and can be removed at any time by clearing the site data in your browser.

## Analytics

We may use third-party analytics services, such as Google Analytics or Baidu Tongji, to collect anonymous visit data: which pages are viewed, roughly where visitors come from, and what kind of device and browser they use. We use this to see which pages are useful and which are not.

These services are run by other companies and have their own privacy policies. They may set cookies or use similar techniques. If you would rather not be counted, you can block them with your browser's privacy settings or with a content-blocking extension. The site works the same either way.

## Server logs

The company that hosts this site keeps routine access logs, as nearly all hosting providers do. These typically include the IP address, the time of the request, the page requested and the browser's user agent. They are used for operating and protecting the service.

## Links to other sites

Plugin pages and tutorials link to GitHub and other third-party websites. Once you follow a link, that site's privacy policy applies, not this one.

## Email

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it to reply and to handle your request.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
