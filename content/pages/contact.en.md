## How to reach us

Email is the only channel we use: [{email}](mailto:{email}).

## What to write to us about

- Submitting a plugin, or asking for a listing to be corrected or removed.
- Errors on the site: an outdated description, a wrong category, a broken link or a mistake in a tutorial.
- Working together, for example on tutorials or community projects.

It helps if you include the address of the page or repository you are writing about.

## What we cannot help with

We do not provide technical support for DeepSeek Harness itself. {brand} is a community site and is not part of the official project. For bugs, installation problems or feature requests, open an issue in the [official repository](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness). For problems with a specific plugin, contact its author through that plugin's repository.
